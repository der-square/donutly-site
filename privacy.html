Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Applies to the Donutly iOS app

Donutly is built to keep your money information on your own device. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. "We" means Choy Hok Lam, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for (the "controller" of) personal data handled through Donutly. You can contact us at littlesquare000@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The money information you enter (for example transactions, categories, bills, budget and savings settings) is stored locally on your iPhone in the app's own database. We do not have an account system, we do not run a server that receives this information, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted from your device. If you use device backups (such as iCloud Backup), your device may include the app's data in them; that is controlled by Apple and your settings. Deleting the app does not delete copies in backups or files you have exported.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No sign-up, name, email address or phone number is required to use Donutly.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDK. The only third-party service built into the app is RevenueCat (see section 3).
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • If you turn on Face ID lock, authentication is handled by iOS. Donutly does not receive or store your biometric data.

3. Purchases (Donutly Pro) and RevenueCat

Donutly Pro is an optional subscription bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never receive your card or payment details. To check which features you have unlocked, show prices and let you restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription-management service that processes this information on our behalf as our "processor". The app contacts RevenueCat when you open it, even if you have not bought Donutly Pro.

RevenueCat receives: a random ID created for this installation of the app; your Apple purchase information (which Donutly Pro plan you have, and its transaction and renewal status); and technical data that comes with the connection, such as your IP address, device type, iOS version and app version. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your email address or any of the money information you enter in Donutly. This information is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is not sold. We look at RevenueCat's combined subscription statistics, such as how many people subscribe, to run and improve the app.

RevenueCat stores this data in the United States, on Amazon Web Services. Under RevenueCat's data processing terms, it must process the data only on our instructions and keep it secure, and transfers from the UK are covered by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. We only share data with third parties that protect it to at least the standard described in this policy. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Why we use this information and how long it is kept

We use the information described in section 3 only to provide and restore Donutly Pro, show prices, and keep purchases secure. Under UK data protection law, our legal basis is that this is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the app and Donutly Pro). For combined subscription statistics, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running and improving the app. RevenueCat keeps this record for as long as we use RevenueCat for Donutly, unless you ask us to delete it sooner (see section 6). If we stop using RevenueCat, we will ask it to delete the data. If you email us, we keep your email only as long as needed to deal with your request and delete it within 12 months.

5. Exporting your data

If you use the export feature (CSV or Excel), the file is created on your device and you choose where to share or save it. We do not receive it. Once you share it, the file is handled by the app or service you choose, under its own terms.

6. Your choices and rights

Your money information stays on your device: you can view, edit or delete it in the app, or delete the app. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Wherever you live, you can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of personal data that we or RevenueCat hold about you, and you can object to our use of it. Because we do not know who you are, please include your Apple order ID (shown on your Apple receipt email) so that we can try to find the record. If we cannot find a record from the details you give, we will tell you. We will reply within one month. In the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In Hong Kong you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (pcpd.org.hk).

7. Children

Donutly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes (for example, if we add sign-in, sync, analytics or advertising), we will update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect. If a change means the app will collect new kinds of personal data or use it for new purposes, we will also tell you in the app before it happens and, where the law requires (for example, for advertising identifiers or for tracking under Apple's App Tracking Transparency), ask for your consent first. Earlier versions are available on request.

9. Contact

私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 16 日 · 適用於 Donutly iOS app

Donutly 的設計原則是把你的金錢資料留在你自己的裝置上。本政策說明本 app 會收集及不會收集甚麼。「我們」指身處英國的個人開發者 Choy Hok Lam，即透過 Donutly 處理的個人資料的負責人（「資料控制者」／「資料使用者」）。聯絡電郵：littlesquare000@gmail.com。

1. 儲存在你裝置上的資料

你輸入的金錢資料（例如交易、類別、帳單、預算及儲蓄設定）儲存在你 iPhone 上 app 自己的資料庫。我們沒有帳戶系統，也沒有接收這些資料的伺服器，我們看不到你的資料。刪除 app 後，這些資料會從你的裝置移除。如你使用裝置備份（例如 iCloud 備份），裝置可能會把 app 資料包括在備份內，這由 Apple 及你的設定決定。刪除 app 不會刪除備份內的副本或你已匯出的檔案。

2. 我們不收集的資料

  • 使用 Donutly 不需要註冊，也不需要提供姓名、電郵或電話號碼。
  • 本 app 沒有加入任何第三方分析、廣告、當機報告或追蹤 SDK。本 app 內置的唯一第三方服務是 RevenueCat（見第 3 節）。
  • 我們不會出售你的資料，也不會把資料用於廣告。
  • 如你開啟 Face ID 鎖，驗證由 iOS 處理，Donutly 不會取得或儲存你的生物特徵資料。

3. 購買（Donutly Pro）及 RevenueCat

Donutly Pro 是透過 Apple 購買的可選訂閱。付款由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得你的信用卡或付款資料。為確認你已解鎖的功能、顯示價錢及讓你還原購買，本 app 使用訂閱管理服務 RevenueCat, Inc.，由其代我們處理有關資料（即我們的「資料處理者」）。即使你沒有購買 Donutly Pro，你開啟本 app 時，本 app 亦會連接 RevenueCat。

RevenueCat 會收到：為此裝置上的 app 隨機產生的識別碼；你的 Apple 購買資料（例如你的 Donutly Pro 方案及其交易和續期狀態）；以及連線時附帶的技術資料，例如 IP 位址、裝置類型、iOS 版本及 app 版本。RevenueCat 不會收到你的姓名、電郵地址或你在 Donutly 輸入的任何金錢資料。這些資料不會與你的身分連結，不會用作追蹤或廣告，亦不會出售。我們會查看 RevenueCat 提供的整體訂閱統計（例如訂閱人數），以營運及改善本 app。

RevenueCat 把資料儲存在美國的 Amazon Web Services。根據 RevenueCat 的資料處理條款，RevenueCat 只可按我們的指示處理資料並須確保資料安全；由英國轉移的資料受英國《國際資料轉移附錄》（International Data Transfer Addendum）保障。我們只會與提供不低於本政策所述保障水平的第三方分享資料。RevenueCat 私隱政策：revenuecat.com/privacy。

4. 使用目的、法律依據及保留期限

第 3 節所述的資料只會用於提供及還原 Donutly Pro、顯示價錢，以及確保購買安全。根據英國資料保障法例，我們的法律依據是履行與你訂立的合約（提供本 app 及 Donutly Pro）。整體訂閱統計則基於我們營運及改善本 app 的正當利益。只要我們繼續為 Donutly 使用 RevenueCat，RevenueCat 便會保留有關紀錄，除非你要求我們提早刪除（見第 6 節）。如我們停止使用 RevenueCat，我們會要求其刪除有關資料。如你以電郵聯絡我們，我們只會在處理你的要求所需的期間保留該電郵，並會在 12 個月內刪除。

5. 匯出資料

如你使用匯出功能（CSV 或 Excel），檔案會在你的裝置上建立，由你選擇分享或儲存的位置，我們不會收到該檔案。檔案分享後，會由你選擇的 app 或服務按其條款處理。

6. 你的選擇及權利

你的金錢資料留在你的裝置上：你可在 app 內查看、修改或刪除，或刪除本 app。訂閱可在 iPhone「設定」›「你的名稱」›「訂閱項目」管理或取消。

不論你身處何地，你都可以要求查閱、更正或刪除我們或 RevenueCat 持有與你有關的個人資料，亦可反對我們使用該等資料。由於我們不知道你的身分，請在電郵中提供你的 Apple 訂單編號（見 Apple 收據電郵），方便我們嘗試找出有關紀錄。如我們憑你提供的資料找不到紀錄，我們會告知你。我們會在一個月內回覆。英國用戶可向資訊專員辦公室（Information Commissioner's Office，ico.org.uk）投訴；香港用戶可向個人資料私隱專員公署（pcpd.org.hk）投訴。

7. 兒童

Donutly 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們亦不會刻意收集他們的個人資料。

8. 政策更新

如本 app 處理資料的方式有任何改變（例如加入登入、同步、分析或廣告功能），我們會在改變生效前更新本政策及上方日期。如改變涉及收集新類別的個人資料或作新用途，我們亦會在改變前於 app 內通知你，並在法例要求時（例如使用廣告識別碼，或 Apple「App 追蹤透明度」所涵蓋的追蹤）先徵求你的同意。如需舊版本，可向我們索取。
