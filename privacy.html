Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · Applies to the Donutly iOS app

Donutly is built to keep your money information on your own device. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. "We" means Choy Hok Lam, the developer of Donutly, based in the United Kingdom.

1. Information stored on your device

The money information you enter (for example transactions, categories, bills, budget and savings settings) is stored locally on your iPhone in the app's own database. We do not have an account system, we do not run a server that receives this information, and we cannot see it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted from your device. If you use device backups (such as iCloud Backup), your device may include the app's data in them; that is controlled by Apple and your settings.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No sign-up, name, email address or phone number is required to use Donutly.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking software is included in the app.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • If you turn on Face ID lock, authentication is handled by iOS. Donutly does not receive or store your biometric data.

3. Purchases (Donutly Pro)

Donutly Pro is an optional subscription bought through Apple. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm which features you have unlocked, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information (such as which Donutly Pro plan you have and its status) linked to an anonymous identifier generated for the app installation, together with standard technical information that accompanies any internet request. This is not linked to your name, and we do not use it for tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Exporting your data

If you use the export feature (CSV or Excel), the file is created on your device and you choose where to share or save it. We do not receive it.

5. Your choices and rights

Because your money information stays on your device, you can view, edit or delete it directly in the app, or by deleting the app. Subscriptions are managed in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. If you are in the UK or EEA, you have rights over personal data under data protection law (such as access, correction and deletion). For any request about information we hold, contact us at the address below.

6. Children

Donutly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, if we add sign-in, sync or analytics), we will update this policy and the date above before the change takes effect.

8. Contact

私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 13 日 · 適用於 Donutly iOS app

Donutly 的設計原則是把你的金錢資料留在你自己的裝置上。本政策說明本 app 會收集及不會收集甚麼。「我們」指 Donutly 的開發者 Choy Hok Lam（設於英國）。

1. 儲存在你裝置上的資料

你輸入的金錢資料（例如交易、類別、帳單、預算及儲蓄設定）只儲存在你 iPhone 上 app 自己的資料庫。我們沒有帳戶系統，也沒有接收這些資料的伺服器，我們看不到你的資料。刪除 app 後，這些資料會從你的裝置移除。如你使用裝置備份（例如 iCloud 備份），裝置可能會把 app 資料包括在備份內，這由 Apple 及你的設定決定。

2. 我們不收集的資料

  • 使用 Donutly 不需要註冊，也不需要提供姓名、電郵或電話號碼。
  • 本 app 沒有加入任何分析、廣告或追蹤軟件。
  • 我們不會出售你的資料，也不會把資料用於廣告。
  • 如你開啟 Face ID 鎖，驗證由 iOS 處理，Donutly 不會取得或儲存你的生物特徵資料。

3. 購買（Donutly Pro）

Donutly Pro 是透過 Apple 購買的可選訂閱。付款完全由 Apple 處理，我們看不到你的信用卡或付款資料。為了確認你已解鎖的功能，本 app 使用訂閱管理服務 RevenueCat。RevenueCat 會接收購買及訂閱資料（例如你的 Donutly Pro 方案及狀態），並連結至為本 app 安裝而產生的匿名識別碼，以及任何網絡請求附帶的標準技術資料。這些資料不會連結至你的姓名，我們亦不會用作追蹤。請參閱 RevenueCat 的私隱政策：revenuecat.com/privacy。

4. 匯出資料

如你使用匯出功能（CSV 或 Excel），檔案會在你的裝置上建立，由你選擇分享或儲存的位置，我們不會收到該檔案。

5. 你的選擇及權利

由於你的金錢資料留在裝置上，你可以直接在 app 內查看、修改或刪除，或透過刪除 app 來移除。訂閱可在 iPhone「設定」›「你的名稱」›「訂閱項目」管理。如你位於英國或歐洲經濟區，你依據資料保障法例對個人資料享有權利（例如查閱、更正及刪除）。如對我們持有的資料有任何要求，請按下方聯絡方式與我們聯絡。

6. 兒童

Donutly 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們亦不會刻意收集他們的個人資料。

7. 政策更新

如 app 的資料處理方式有所改變（例如加入登入、同步或分析功能），我們會在改變生效前更新本政策及上方日期。
